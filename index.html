Project Details

Stabler-Leadbeater Apothecary AR Project

This project is an exhibit for the Alexandria Stabler-Leadbeater Apothecary museum, to demonstrate an application of AR into their museum.

Notes:

  • A working webcam/phone camera is needed to view the AR content.
  • A “hiro” preset marker is required to display the AR content.
  • 3D models are displayed using the .glb file format.

Help

Steps:

  1. Click on a medicine
  2. Enable camera / motion tracking on your phone
  3. Point your camera at the "Hiro" text on the marker
  4. Make sure to keep the entire marker in view of your phone camera
  5. Keep your phone roughly a foot away

Quick Fixes:

  • Make sure camera acess is enabled in your phone setting.
  • Make sure that motion tracking is enabled in your phone settings.
  • Make sure that your phone supports javascript.
